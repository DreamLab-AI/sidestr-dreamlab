sidestr:dreamlab

Block mirror for DreamLab's experimental sidestr sidechain beside Bitcoin testnet4. Coins here carry no value.

  • chain.json: the chain document
  • blocks.json: the block index
  • blocks.dat: the raw blocks

Protocol: sidestr/spec by Melvin Carvalho. Rust implementation: sidestr-rs. Estate: visionflow.info.
